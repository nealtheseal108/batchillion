How to play

  1. 7 prompts a day, the same for everyone.
  2. Each prompt asks for a BatCH teammate who started university (anywhere) in a given year (the first is 2021 or before). The last is a wildcard: anyone who ever played.
  3. You get 40 seconds per prompt. Press enter to throw. A miss clears the box and the clock keeps running. In Players, keep trying until you catch one. On stat sheets you get 3 to 5 guesses per prompt (more when fewer teammates fit), and running out ends that throw. Only your first round on each stat sheet counts on its leaderboard; replay it as much as you like for practice.
  4. Only an exact full name catches. A first name, or a full name with one typo, gets filled in on the first enter; press enter again to throw. If two teammates share a first name, it fills in the lower-tier one, so type the full name to get the other. Partial names are never completed. When time runs out, a correct name still in the box counts; anything else is a turnover.
  5. The rarer the pick, the farther the throw. Points are yards: a one in a batchillion goes 100, all the way into the end zone. Your score is how far you threw it in total. The teammate everyone thinks is a sneaky deep pick? Everyone else thought of them too: too clever, 15.
  6. Players rounds: once you name a teammate, they're gone for you in Players. Not tomorrow, not ever. Who counts: for anyone who started university before 2024, only teammates who played in a USA Ultimate sanctioned tournament. Stat sheet rounds: each name counts once per round (naming someone twice gets 0 the second time). Next round you can use anyone again, and stat sheets never use up your Players names.
  7. Typed someone who isn't on the roster? The last one you tried is kept. You get one appeal a day after the round; Neal ranks it and your score updates.
  8. Play as many rounds as you want. Players rounds drain your pool of names; stat sheets never do. The leaderboard is public and ranks single rounds: your best round counts, not your total.
