HOW TO PLAY

  1. 7 prompts a day. Same for everyone.
  2. Each one asks for a BatCH teammate from a starting class. The last is a wildcard: anyone ever.
  3. 25 seconds to name one. First name is enough unless two people share it.
  4. Rare teammates fly farther. Points are yards. 100 is the end zone.
  5. Someone missing? Hit appeal. Neal ranks them and your score updates.
  6. One huck session per day.
