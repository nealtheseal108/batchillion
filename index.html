How to play

  1. 7 prompts a day, the same for everyone.
  2. Each prompt asks for a BatCH teammate who started at UNC in a given year (the first is 2021 or before). The last is a wildcard: anyone who ever played.
  3. You get 30 seconds per prompt. Press enter to throw. A miss clears the box and the clock keeps running, so keep trying until you catch one.
  4. Only an exact full name catches. A first name, or a full name with one typo, gets filled in on the first enter; press enter again to throw. If two teammates share a first name, it fills in the lower-tier one, so type the full name to get the other. Partial names are never completed. When time runs out, a correct name still in the box counts; anything else is a turnover.
  5. The rarer the pick, the deeper the huck. Each throw starts where the last one was caught, and every 100 points is a goal. The teammate everyone thinks is a sneaky deep pick? Everyone else thought of them too: too clever, 15.
  6. Once you use a teammate, they're gone for you. Not tomorrow, not ever.
  7. Typed someone who isn't on the roster? The last one you tried is kept. You get one appeal a day after the round; Neal ranks it and your score updates.
  8. One round per person per day. The leaderboard is public.
